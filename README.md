# Frameglass

Open the app: **https://bertbays-source.github.io/frameglass/** (Edge or Chrome)

Frameglass is a browser-based video viewer for drone footage. It can:

- Play MP4, WebM and MOV video with frame stepping, looks, color adjustments and sharpening
- Zoom up to 64×, compare before/after, and check exposure with zebras and a histogram
- Highlight look-alike objects in the frame
- Read the drone's GPS position for every frame from DJI video files (or a DJI .SRT log)
- Show the flight on a satellite or topo map
- Save any frame as a geotagged JPEG, plus a Google Earth .kmz for the frame or the whole flight

Videos are opened from your own computer and are never uploaded.
